⚠ Important context on Omega-6 for daily cooking families
At 50.38% linoleic acid (Omega-6), this is a high-omega-6 oil. The modern Indian diet is already disproportionately high in omega-6 relative to omega-3. Regular daily use of high-linoleic sunflower oil can worsen the omega-6:omega-3 imbalance, which is associated with increased inflammation. This is not a safety concern — it's a nutrition context: using this oil in moderation, or rotating with oils lower in omega-6 (mustard, coconut, ghee), is the nutritionally optimal approach. The oil itself is clean and safe; the issue is dietary pattern, not product safety.
