🌿 Herbs of Concern — What's in This Product

Herbal ingredients: green tea extract, curcumin, Garcinia cambogia.

Herbal extracts in protein supplements can be a source of pesticide residues and heavy metals. Long-term safety data for many herbal combinations in supplements is limited. Consult a doctor if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or on medication.
HerbWhy it matters
Garcinia cambogiaWeight-management herb linked to liver toxicity in some studies — caution for daily use, pregnancy or those on medication
Green tea extractConcentrated catechins have been associated with liver stress at high doses
Curcumin (turmeric)Generally safe; may interact with blood thinners and some medications
