Delete your ChoosePure account

Enter the email associated with your ChoosePure account. We'll send you a confirmation link. Your account and personal data will be permanently deleted within 24 hours of confirming.

For legal and accounting reasons, anonymised payment records (amount, date and transaction IDs) are retained with no personal identifiers in our systems. Our payment processor (Razorpay) retains its own transaction records under its privacy policy.

Prefer in-app? You can also request deletion from the app: Profile → Request account deletion.

Signed up with a phone number? Email support@choosepure.in with your registered phone number and we'll process your request within 24 hours.
